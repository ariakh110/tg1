# Update CRM customer profiles and due reminders

Record the contact name, role in the organization and final decision authority from mobile call intake. Allow editing the same profile from the website and mobile CRM with their existing permission scopes. Send due work reminders through the current Bale bot to the verified private identity of the responsible CRM operator. Keep the public price ticker grouped by factory and product type and ordered numerically by size.

The customer and call capture fields are additive. The existing purchase inquiry timer also processes a bounded batch of due CRM reminders. The ticker uses a lightweight public product summary action.
