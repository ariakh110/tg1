# 11 — Social Media & Influencer Marketing

## Social media & SEO: indirect, not direct
- Google has **no algorithm** that counts social followers/engagement as a direct ranking factor.
- **All user links on social platforms are nofollow / UGC → pass no authority.**
- **Indirect benefits** (real and valuable):
  - Google **crawls** social links → faster **discovery/indexing** of your content.
  - More exposure → more chance someone links to you from a **followed** link elsewhere.
  - **Brand mentions + sentiment** around the web influence SEO (Gary Illyes, Pubcon 2017: "the context in which you engage online… can impact what you rank for"). Quality Raters assess brand authority.
  - Longer content lifespan, distribution, thought-leadership positioning.
- **Optimize social profiles** (keywords, description) — they get indexed and rank. **Pinterest** pins/boards rank well for **long-tail** queries (boards with active followers rank better).

## Platform demographics (choose where your audience is)
- Different platforms skew by age/gender/interest. Facebook, YouTube, Instagram = largest audiences; **TikTok** skews younger; **Pinterest** skews female.
- Research method: Google "`<platform> demographics`" (use **recent** studies); search your topics/hashtags on each platform; find **niche networks** (e.g., Fishbrain for fishing).
- Building a social audience takes major effort/time and starts slow — evaluate whether the investment is worth it now.

## Building relationships with influencers
- **37%** of consumers trust influencers more than brands (Oracle/CRM Essentials, 2022); Gen Z/Millennials ~2× more likely to trust influencer content.
- Influencers are an **accelerant**: their audience is larger and they have more influence per person (more shares/links from the same audience size). One influencer with 20k connections > a group cumulatively with 20k.
- Keys to getting a share: content must be **relevant to them**, **excellent** (not just good), they must **trust/know you**, they must **see it** (a single share can vanish in minutes), and it must be **unique/distinct**.
- **Process (be patient, sequential):**
  1. Right mindset: relationship is **mutually beneficial**; never treat them as targets.
  2. Study what they share/care about (professional + personal interests).
  3. Add value: reshare *some* of their content, add **meaningful** comments, engage their other readers/fans, help the broader community (not just the influencer — over-focus looks like stalking).
  4. Leverage warm intros / conferences (meet face-to-face) — but don't move too fast.
  - Higher-value relationships justify higher effort.

## Targeting influencers on social platforms
- **Facebook groups** — join active, relevant groups; become an engaged participant. **Boost posts** to expand reach beyond the small organic subset.
- Tools: **HYPR** (audience makeup), Facebook **Brand Collabs Manager**, social-listening tools (find who mentions your/competitor brands), influencer marketplaces (**GRIN**, Constant Contact).
- Concepts apply across networks; pick platforms by demographic fit.

## Connecting with the media (a special class of influencer)
- Media + topical bloggers give **citable** mentions/links of high value. Targets: NYT, WSJ, Washington Post, TechCrunch, Mashable — **plus vertical-specific authorities** (e.g., thisoldhouse.com, bobvila.com for home improvement) which are as authoritative for your market due to relevance.
- **Pitching rules:** great relevant subject line; short pitch (4–5 paragraphs); establish authority fast; include bullet points of the most interesting findings; **data-driven** pieces pitch best; first pitch must be on-point (a bad first pitch can get you blacklisted); cultivate over time.

## Partnerships
Co-marketing with brands/data providers (Homesick × Lucasfilm; GoPro × Red Bull; Backlinko × Semrush). Like influencer relationships: both parties bring value, which is what makes them powerful.

## Case-study takeaways
TikTok (Crocs #ThousandDollarCrocs, NBA), YouTube (Sitecore Santa CX for B2B; AGCO for B2B), Instagram (Best Made Co), cause campaigns (Virgin Atlantic "dyslexic thinking" — +1,562% positive sentiment, 250+ outlets, tons of links). Social visibility lift → drives **organic search** traffic too.
