# 01 — SEO Fundamentals

## Definition
**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** = the practice of improving a website's visibility in organic (unpaid) search results. It is one discipline inside a larger digital-marketing strategy and is focused on **free organic traffic** (as opposed to SEM/paid search).

Related digital-marketing disciplines an SEO must coordinate with:
- **SEM** — bidding for paid ads on search engines.
- **Social Media Marketing** — free + paid social engagement.
- **Content Marketing** — blogs, newsletters, editorial content.
- **PR** — relationships + newsworthy content.
- **CRO** (conversion rate optimization) and **UX**.

## The three pillars of SEO
1. **On-page (on-site) optimization** — elements on a page/group of pages: content, keyword choice, metadata (title tags, meta descriptions), headings.
2. **Technical (on-site) optimization** — how well search engines can crawl/understand the site: code, site structure, sitemaps, robots.txt, errors, redirects, speed.
3. **Off-page (off-site) optimization** — actions outside your site: link building + brand recognition + social. Historically = acquiring links; now also brand/social signals.

**Recommended order of work:** Technical review → content & keyword recommendations → off-page strategy. Without a sound technical foundation, content may never be discovered.

## White hat vs. black hat vs. gray hat
- **White hat** — follows search-engine guidelines; sustainable. (This KB teaches white hat.)
- **Black hat** — manipulative; "churn and burn"; gets penalized/banned more often.
- **Gray hat** — in between.

## Search engines & market share
- Major: **Google** (largest share), Yahoo, Bing.
- International: **Baidu** (China), **Yandex** (Russia), **Naver** (South Korea).
- "We're not search-engine-optimization experts, we're **Google-optimization** experts." Optimizing for Google = following best practices that help everywhere.
- Reminder: Google is a **for-profit** company; ads fund it. Algorithm changes serve user retention (so the ad business stays healthy).

## Why ranking position matters (click distribution)
- ~**71%** of all traffic comes from **page 1** of results.
- The **first five organic results** capture ~**68%** of clicks.
- Users refine their query rather than go to page 2.
- Moving up a few positions can boost traffic significantly → small gains matter.

## SEO as a career
- Now a **core marketing skill**. Roles: consultant, agency, in-house (small biz / startup / enterprise).
- Complements content marketing, social, CRO, UX, PR.
- Core competencies: tie recommendations to business goals; estimate value; back claims with data/case studies; prioritize.
- Practice + continual testing is the best teacher. Stay current via SEO blogs, conferences, forums; analyze Google patents (e.g., Bill Slawski's *SEO by the Sea*).

## Foundational ranking model
A page ranks based on a combination of:
- **Relevance** to the query (content + comprehensiveness, including likely follow-up questions).
- **Authority** (largely links, plus brand/trust signals).
- **Technical accessibility** (crawlable, indexable).
- Increasingly: **user experience**, **brand/trust**, and **intent match**.

Ranking factors are grouped into: **on-page**, **off-site** (links, brand mentions), and **domain/site-level** factors. Google uses **200+** signals and ships **500+** updates per year (most unannounced).
