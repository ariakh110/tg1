# 12 — App SEO & ASO (App Store Optimization)

## Two distinct practices
- **ASO (App Store Optimization)** — optimizing your app **inside** the app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) so it ranks for category/keyword searches there.
- **External App SEO** — optimizing your **website / other properties** to drive traffic to the app from outside the stores (often called ASEO).

## Why both matter (ASO benefits)
- ~**35–40%** uplift in organic downloads over time; **65%** of apps are discovered via App Store search.
- Organic users' **LTV** is **3–5×** that of paid. Better conversion lowers paid CAC.
- External SEO: saves on store **commission fees** (Apple/Google take **15–30%**, decreasing with tenure — e.g., Netflix drives signups to its site), captures organic traffic, builds brand awareness (App Store homepages can now rank in organic search too).

## ASO ranking elements (where keywords matter)
- **App name/title** — most important; a keyword in the title gave ~**10.3%** ranking increase (Mobile Dev/Tune). Structure: **brand/app name first, then relevant keywords.** Use available character space.
- **Category**, **description** (short + long), **keyword tags** (iOS only — Apple relies on tags and does **not** crawl the description; Google **does** crawl the description for keywords).
- **Description:** up to **4,000 characters** in both stores. Apple shows a shortened version + subtitle; Google Play shows a custom short description. Incorporate keywords **naturally** + CTAs + social proof; don't over-optimize (scares users). Customize per store (different users + Apple shortening).
- **Keyword research for apps:** main/popular features, synonyms, what similar successful apps use, right categories, terms people search within the category. Use app-specific tools + traditional keyword tools (test in stores).
- **Reviews/ratings:** higher rating → more relevant → higher rank. **80%** of users read ≥1 review before downloading. Reply to reviews; consider feature requests.
- **Engagement/quality signals** (no direct control): retention, uninstall rate, crash rate. Buggy/scammy/infrequently-updated apps rank lower / get removed. **Update frequently.**
- **Downloads ↔ rank** correlate (users look at ~first 5). **Localize** for international audiences (visibility + trust + more downloads).

## External App SEO methods
- **Must have a website** for the app (many app-only companies lose huge traffic; users still prefer to purchase on web).
- **Content marketing** funneling to the app (Fishbrain writes fishing guides → ties to app).
- **Dedicated landing pages per feature** (Fishbrain feature pages → +5% signup conversion).
- Target **category + "reviews"** keywords (reviews = low volume, low competition, high conversion).
- Turn an app-only feature into a **limited web tool** to attract the right audience (Fishbrain's fishing-spots database on web → converted visitors to app users).
- **Participate in communities** (Reddit, forums) where your app/category/competitors are discussed.

## A/B testing (app optimization)
- Split audience between variants; measure which performs best over time (can use 3+ variants split evenly).
- Even testing the **app icon** alone has lifted conversions by up to **25%**. Test: icon, screenshots (text/color/layout/order), title, video, descriptions, emojis, social proof, description format.
- **Always start with a hypothesis** (what changes, expected result, why). Example: Fishbrain highlighting privacy of fishing spots → ~**17%** download increase.
- **Google Play Console** has built-in testing (auto-segments, flags statistical significance, ~**90%** confidence interval). **Apple doesn't allow store testing** — use your own landing page, ad messaging, or third-party mock-up tools (less reliable: behavior differs across platforms).
- **First two screenshots** are by far the most viewed (few scroll past). Don't discard losing tests — they reveal audience behavior (Fishbrain's marketplace tests lost → only promote shop near shopping holidays). Always run follow-up tests to confirm.
