# 09 — Local SEO

## What it is
Optimizing your site + business listings so you appear for **location-relevant** queries. Many queries trigger local results **without** a city name — "florists near me," "ramen," "burgers," "tax accountants," "lawyers." Google infers local intent.

## Goal: two visibility slots
1. **Local Pack** — the map + business list at the top of results.
2. **Organic results** — the normal blue links below.
Appearing in **both** maximizes page-1 real estate and pushes competitors down. Achieving both requires optimizing your **Google Business Profile** (formerly Google My Business) **and** your website.

## Google Business Profile (GBP / "Google My Business")
- **Free.** Sign up and **verify** physical presence (phone call or mailed postcard) before the listing goes live.
- Features: reviews, opening hours, busy-times, products, posts/announcements, **Q&A**, images. (Google changes features often.)
- **Fill out every field.** Most important: **address, opening hours, store images, social + website links.**

## NAP consistency
- **NAP = Name, Address, Phone number.** Must be **accurate and identical** everywhere online.
- Google cross-references your site, other listings, and your GBP. **Discrepancies hurt ranking.** Fix outdated info across the web so everything matches.

## Reviews (one of the most important local factors)
- Review **stars** boost CTR; signal legitimacy; provide social proof; help ranking (esp. when users filter by highest rated); answer customer questions.
- **63%** of consumers check Google reviews before visiting a business; Google holds ~**57.5%** of all online reviews → people review on Google first.
- **Proactively requesting** reviews → higher average rating (~**4.34★** vs **3.89★** for unprompted) and more 5-star, steadier ratings. Unprompted reviews skew toward 1-star over time.
- **Never** offer discounts/items in exchange for reviews → Google may remove all reviews or ban the listing. Follow Google's review solicitation rules.
- **Respond to all reviews** (positive + negative). You can't delete negatives, but you can resolve issues and ask the customer to update; **flag** fake/policy-violating reviews.
- Google tests AI answers in Q&A by matching question keywords to **review** keywords → reviews + product feeds matter more.

## Links & citations
- **Link** = a site links to your site/GBP. **Citation** = a mention of your business NAP (name + maybe address/phone). Both help local SEO. Citations dipped in emphasis but still useful — treat as best practice.

## Website optimization for local
- Add a **store finder** / a dedicated **landing page per physical location**; or a "find us" page with full contact info if single-location.
- Mark up contact info with **schema** (schema.org) so Google maps it to your GBP.
- **Link each GBP listing to its location landing page** (trust + authority).

## Enterprise / multi-location
- Ensure consistent NAP across all locations; storefront photo per location; **2–3 relevant categories** per profile.
- Use enterprise tools (Moz Local, BrightLocal, GetFiveStars, plus Yelp/TripAdvisor) to manage listings, reviews, citations at scale.
- Operational best practices: response templates for support reps (lightly customized to avoid looking canned); **monthly editorial calendar** for posts/Q&A/photos; train in-store + phone + social staff to resolve issues before they become negative reviews and to **ask happy customers to leave Google reviews**; install in-store signage routing complaints to a help line; pursue **local PR** (sponsorships/events → links to site/GBP); use social listening for sentiment.
