# 07 — Measurement in 2026 (new KPIs for the AI-search era)

The course's KPIs (organic traffic, rankings, CTR, links/authority, conversions, ROI — see `../knowledge-base/13_metrics-kpis-roi.md`) **still apply**, but AI search forces new metrics because **impressions no longer map cleanly to clicks**.

## New / evolved KPIs
- **AI Overview presence & citation rate** — for your target queries, does an AIO appear, and are **you** cited? Track which queries trigger AIO and whether you're a source.
- **AI share of voice / LLM visibility** — how often your brand is mentioned/cited across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot for your topic set. (Emerging tools: Profound, Peec AI, Otterly, SE Ranking / Semrush / Ahrefs AI-visibility features, LLMrefs, and similar.)
- **Branded search lift** — AI answers drive awareness without clicks; rising branded search/direct traffic is a key downstream signal that AI visibility is working.
- **Referral traffic from AI engines** — sessions arriving from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot, etc. (segment in analytics).
- **Zero-click / impression-to-click ratio** — expect informational queries to show high impressions, lower clicks; judge them by assist/branded effect, not raw clicks.
- **Conversions & assisted conversions** — double down on outcome metrics (the course's advice to report business outcomes is *more* important now).

## What to de-emphasize
- **Raw organic traffic alone** on informational queries (AIO suppresses clicks) — interpret in context.
- **Single-keyword rank obsession** — still a barometer, but a #1 rank may sit *below* an AIO. Pair with AIO/citation tracking.

## Reporting guidance (extends course §14)
- Add an "AI visibility" section to client reports: AIO appearance %, citation count, AI-engine referrals, branded-search trend.
- Frame the narrative: "We may see fewer clicks per impression on informational queries, but we're being **cited as the authority** and **branded demand is rising**, which converts."
- Keep **year-over-year** comparison and benchmarking discipline from the course.

## Tooling note
Classic tools remain (Search Console, GA4, Semrush, Ahrefs, Moz, Screaming Frog, Lighthouse). Add **AI-visibility trackers** and check **Search Console's AI Overview / AI Mode reporting** as Google expands it.
