# 03 — E-E-A-T in 2026 (the extra "E" + expansion)

## What changed vs. the course
The course teaches **E-A-T** (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Two updates since:

1. **The extra "E" = Experience** (added by Google to the Quality Rater Guidelines, Dec 2022). Now **E-E-A-T**:
   - **Experience** — does the creator have **first-hand, real-world experience** with the topic? (Used the product, visited the place, lived the situation.) This directly targets generic AI-regurgitated content.
   - Expertise, Authoritativeness, **Trust** (Trust is the center/most important member of the family, per Google).

2. **Expansion beyond YMYL.** E-A-T originally hit hardest on **health/finance (YMYL)**. By the **December 2025 core update**, E-E-A-T is applied to **practically all competitive searches** — e-commerce reviews, SaaS comparisons, how-to guides, etc. Trust/experience signals now matter almost everywhere.

## How to demonstrate Experience (the new E)
- First-person accounts, original photos/videos you took, hands-on testing, original data/case studies, "we tested X for N weeks" framing.
- Author bios that establish *lived* experience, not just credentials.
- Avoid generic, synthesizable content with no first-hand signal — it's the exact target of the experience emphasis (and of AI Overviews choosing *other* sources).

## How to demonstrate the rest (still valid from the course, reinforced)
- **Expertise:** high-quality content, clear About page, detailed author bio pages (certifications, degrees, other publications).
- **Authoritativeness:** citations/links from press & authoritative sites, speaking gigs, social discussion, branded search volume, Wikipedia/Wikidata presence, entity recognition.
- **Trust:** accurate contact info, return/payment/shipping policies, T&Cs, **HTTPS**, good external reviews (Trustpilot/Yelp/TripAdvisor/BBB), positive brand sentiment.

## AI-content stance (clarified)
Google (John Mueller, Nov 2025): "Our systems don't care if content is created by AI or humans. What matters is whether it's helpful for users." → AI **as a tool** is fine **if** human expertise, experience, and quality control are present. Mass low-effort AI content without experience/expertise is what gets demoted.

## Why E-E-A-T is central to both SEO and GEO/AEO
The same trust/experience/authority signals that satisfy Quality Raters and core updates are what make content **citable by AI engines**. E-E-A-T is the through-line connecting classic ranking and AI-citation visibility.
