# 06 — Entities & the Knowledge Graph (the 2026 differentiator)

## Why entities now matter most
The course introduces **entity association** (Google matching queries to specific people/things) as an emerging idea. By 2026 it's a **primary differentiator**: with E-E-A-T table-stakes everywhere and AI engines synthesizing answers, **entity recognition** is what separates winners.

An **entity** = a uniquely identifiable thing (a brand, person, product, place, concept) that Google/LLMs track in a **knowledge graph** with relationships to other entities. Search and AI engines increasingly reason over entities, not just keywords/strings.

## How to build entity strength
1. **Be a clearly-defined entity.** Consistent name, description, and identity across the web (NAP for local; brand details everywhere).
2. **Structured data that declares relationships.** `Organization` → `founder`/`employee` (`Person`), `sameAs` → official profiles (LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Crunchbase, social). `Person` schema for authors. This lets engines connect your entity to others (the "entity graph" LLMs follow).
3. **Get into authoritative knowledge sources** — Wikipedia/Wikidata presence, Google Knowledge Panel, reputable directories, industry databases.
4. **Topical authority / topic clusters.** Cover a topic comprehensively with interlinked pillar + cluster pages (the course's "domain-level content strategy," now framed as entities + clusters). This signals you're an authority on the *entity/topic*, not just a page.
5. **Corroboration.** The more reputable sources describe your entity consistently, the more confidently engines (and LLMs) cite you.
6. **Author entities.** Tie content to recognizable expert authors with their own entity footprint (bylines, bios, `sameAs`, off-site authorship).

## Connection to GEO/AEO and E-E-A-T
- LLMs **follow the entity graph** when deciding whom to cite (see `02_geo-aeo-optimization.md`). Strong entity definition → more citations.
- Entity authority **is** authoritativeness in E-E-A-T terms — same goal, modern mechanism.

## Practical takeaway for agents
When advising in 2026: don't stop at "target keyword X." Ask "is the client a well-defined **entity** Google and LLMs can recognize, connect, and trust?" Build keyword → topic cluster → **entity** authority.
