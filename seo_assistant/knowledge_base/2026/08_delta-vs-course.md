## What did NOT change (still fully valid — keep teaching these)
- Crawl → index → rank pipeline; relevance + authority + comprehensiveness.
- On-page: title tag ≤60 chars (~55), meta description ~150–160, one H1, keyword-rich URLs, unique valuable content, internal linking.
- Technical: sitemaps, robots.txt, 301 vs 302, soft-404s, redirect chains, status codes.
- Keyword research: intent, stages of search, head vs long-tail (~70% of traffic), buckets, mapping, competitive analysis.
- Content marketing: anchor/data-driven content, skyscraper, partnerships, audience-first.
- Off-page: editorially-given links, link value (logarithmic authority), dos/don'ts, disavow.
- Local SEO: GBP, NAP consistency, reviews, citations.
- Personas, client management, SMART goals, ROI/attribution challenges.

## One-line guidance for agents
> Teach the course fundamentals as the foundation, then **always layer 2026**: optimize to be the **cited, experienced, entity-recognized source** that both classic rankings and AI answers prefer — and **measure AI visibility**, not just clicks.
